Privacy Policy

Passport Photo Maker processes photos on your iPhone. The app has no account, developer-operated server, advertising or analytics SDK.

Photos and camera

You choose the photos the app can use. Apple's photo picker gives the app access to the selected photo without broad photo-library permission. The app requests camera access only when you tap Take photo. Camera captures are prepared as JPEGs for use in the app. You can change camera permission in iPhone Settings.

On-device processing

The app uses Apple image-processing features on your device to crop photos, assess image dimensions and size, detect faces and body features, and optionally replace a background with white. These checks are guidance only. The app does not send photos, face-analysis results or image metadata to a developer-operated server. It does not create an account or use a third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Local storage

Imported and captured photos remain in app memory while you work. If you explicitly save an editable print project, the app stores a copy of the source photo and its crop, size and background settings in its local app storage. Exported images and PDFs are prepared in the app's temporary storage for the iPhone share sheet. Temporary files may remain until iOS clears them or the app is removed. Depending on your device settings, Apple may include local app data in a device backup.

Sharing and external links

Photos leave the app only when you choose to share or save them using the iPhone share sheet or Files. The service or app you select then handles the file under its own privacy terms. The app also links to official photo guidance websites; opening those links connects your browser to those sites.

Retention and deletion

Saved editable projects remain in the app until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete a project from its More options menu. Removing the app removes its local data from that device, but does not remove files you previously shared or saved elsewhere, or backups managed by Apple.

Support and this website

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the public support repository. Do not include photos or personal information in a public GitHub issue. GitHub hosts these support pages and issues; its handling of website and account information is described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The update date above will be revised when that happens.
